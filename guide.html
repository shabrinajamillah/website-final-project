FIRST THINGS FIRST

What is Sky?

Sky: Children of the Light is a peaceful social adventure game where players explore magical realms, help spirits, collect light, and meet other players.

There is no single way to play Sky. You can explore the world, collect cosmetics, complete quests, make friends, or simply enjoy the scenery.

GETTING STARTED

How to Begin

Your journey starts with a few simple mechanics that you will use throughout the game.

🌎

Explore

Travel through different realms, discover new locations, and follow the paths left by ancient spirits.

🪽

Fly

Use your cape to fly across the world. Collect Winged Light to increase your flying ability.

🕯️

Collect

Collect light and candles that can be used for different purposes, including unlocking cosmetics.

👻

Help Spirits

Find spirits hidden throughout the realms and relive their memories to unlock their rewards.

KNOW THE BASICS

Important Things to Know

01

Winged Light

Winged Light increases the level of your cape and allows you to fly for longer periods.

02

Candles

Candles are one of the main currencies in Sky and are commonly collected by gathering light throughout the realms.

03

Spirits

Spirits can be found throughout the realms. Helping them allows you to unlock memories and rewards.

04

Other Players

Sky is designed around social interaction. You can meet, communicate, and explore together with other players.
