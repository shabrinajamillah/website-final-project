POPULAR ACTIVITY

What Is a Candle Run?

A Candle Run, often called CR, is a routine where players travel through different areas to collect light that can be turned into candles.

Candles can then be used to unlock various items and cosmetics from spirits.

01

Travel

Visit different areas.

Turn light into candles.
