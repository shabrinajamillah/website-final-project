THE REALMS

Seven Places to Explore

Every realm offers a different experience during your journey.

01 · STARTING REALM

Isle of Dawn

Isle of Dawn is where every new journey begins. This peaceful desert introduces players to the basic mechanics of Sky and the first spirits.

It is a simple and calm realm that helps players learn how to explore, fly, and interact with the world.

02 · OPEN PLAINS

Daylight Prairie

Daylight Prairie is a bright and open realm filled with grasslands, clouds, and peaceful landscapes.

Players can explore wide areas, discover spirits, collect Winged Light, and meet other players.

03 · MYSTERIOUS FOREST

Hidden Forest

Hidden Forest is a beautiful forest realm surrounded by rain and ancient trees.

Players must explore carefully while protecting their light from the environment.

04 · MOUNTAIN VALLEY

Valley of Triumph

Valley of Triumph is a fast and energetic realm filled with snowy mountains and large racing areas.

It is known for flying, racing, and beautiful views across the mountains.

05 · DANGEROUS LAND

Golden Wasteland

Golden Wasteland is a darker and more dangerous realm. The area contains ruins, storms, and creatures that can threaten your light.

Players need to pay attention to their surroundings while exploring this realm.

06 · ANCIENT KNOWLEDGE

Vault of Knowledge

Vault of Knowledge is a mysterious tower filled with different levels and ancient memories.

Players can explore its floors, discover spirits, and uncover more of the history of Sky.

07 · FINAL JOURNEY

Eye of Eden

Eye of Eden is the final realm of the main journey. It presents the most dangerous environment in Sky.

Players must use what they have learned throughout their journey to face this challenging area.
